# THE SPIRAL — World 05

**The walls begin whispering.**

An immersive gallery world within Overflowism, where thoughts repeat, contradict, intensify, cross a threshold, and become abstract art.

> Experience → Feeling → Threshold → Release → Form

The founding Overflowism statement remains **"I create what I can't contain."** The Spiral is one expression of an artistic movement that also celebrates joy, beauty, spirituality, music, travel, nature, and love. It is not a clinical model or an assessment of the visitor.

## Standalone world

- The four original official worlds remain unchanged: THE WATCHERS, THE NOISE, THE BLOOM, and THE HEART.
- THE SPIRAL is the fifth official world with its **own entrance** at `spiral.html`, separate from THE NOISE or THE HEART.
- THE UNDERCURRENT continues to exist as a hidden connecting layer, not counted among the five official worlds.
- The existing `The Spiral` room inside THE WATCHERS remains intact; it does not replace or stand in for the new world.
- The main atrium links directly to `spiral.html`; the central atlas and world directory link to all thirteen dedicated rooms.
- The complete atlas now contains **62 rooms**: 49 previous entries + thirteen Spiral installations.

## Thirteen connected rooms

1. **THE WHISPERS:** discover hidden thoughts by clicking or touching them; conflicting messages appear.
2. **THE LOOP:** reappearing questions and repetitive symbols become visual patterns.
3. **THE MIRROR:** reflecting thoughts reveal internal confessions and contrasting perspectives.
4. **THE COLLAPSE:** accumulated contradictions transform into a layered abstract canvas; no flashes or aggressive camera shake.
5. **THE STILLNESS:** temporary quiet, warmth and uncertainty, not a forced happy ending.
6. **THE CREATIVE SPIRAL:** multiplying paintlike fragments and colors turn ideas into art.
7. **THE JOY SPIRAL:** discover flowers, butterflies and light as delight expands.
8. **THE LOVE SPIRAL:** follow affectionate threads and floating petal forms.
9. **THE WONDER SPIRAL:** explore original portal motifs and a layered, zoom-like visual response (not yet an infinite free-roaming world).
10. **THE GRATITUDE SPIRAL:** notice small lights and let details become connected.
11. **THE POSSIBILITY SPIRAL:** explore three doors into possible futures, with no guaranteed outcome.
12. **THE BECOMING SPIRAL:** transform architectural shapes, organic forms and the palette as you explore.
13. **THE AFFIRMATION SPIRAL:** discover editable positive affirmations through an interactive mirror, a garden of words, echoes and constellation; optionally carry one thought to other rooms.

## Core interaction

- `spiral-data.js` preserves the five original room descriptions, ten foundational categories, curated opposites, and thresholds.
- `spiral-expansion-data.js` adds eight new rooms, affirmative and playful categories, room connections, room-specific visual motifs, artwork placement slots and the editable array of affirmations.
- `spiral.js` implements the shared whisper engine, overlays, canvas transformation, all thirteen room routes, artwork lookup and optional hum.
- `spiral-expansion.js` adds original interactive scene objects and all five Affirmation Spiral interactions (mirror, garden, echo, constellation and a carryable thought).
- `spiral-expansion.css` provides room-specific light, architectural motifs, dimensional objects, and responsive installations.
- `spiral.css` defines cinematic corridor architecture, fog, mirrored light, text, and responsive motion.
- `spiral.html` is the immersive entrance and accessible interaction UI.

The engine reveals text in varied locations and lets visitors click a thought to uncover an opposite. After enough interactions, its language seeds an original interactive digital abstract composition. The composition is **not presented as one of Marcela's paintings**.

## Artworks

Each of the thirteen rooms includes a gallery alcove and editable slot in `spiral-expansion-data.js`. Until an authentic original work is assigned, it explicitly displays a placeholder. Existing paintings in other worlds are not reused or removed. The abstract canvas is generated by visitor interactions; it must not be presented as one of the artist's original physical paintings.

To assign a future piece, add a non-placeholder entry to `gallery-data.js` with `world:'spiral'` and `room` matching the desired room id: `whispers`, `loop`, `mirror`, `collapse`, `stillness`, `creative`, `joy`, `love`, `wonder`, `gratitude`, `possibility`, `becoming`, or `affirmation` (or use the prefixed `roomId`, such as `spiral-joy`).

## Controls and inclusion

- Desktop: click/tap thought fragments; navigate with room links, Previous / Next buttons, or left/right arrow keys when focus is not on a button.
- Mobile: room navigation and controls remain touch-sized and text remains accessible.
- Pause Motion stops all decorative animation, and `prefers-reduced-motion` disables it by default.
- Sound starts **off** and becomes a subtle synthesized low hum only after explicit opt-in. There are no recorded spoken whispers, and the UI never pretends there are audio files.
- Native artwork dialog, visible focus states, descriptive labels and alternate return-to-gallery links are provided.
- Content is local and curated; no camera permission, diagnosis, visitor profiling, analytics, or visitor-supplied private thoughts.

## The 2026 manifesto still governs

> A spiral doesn't always mean you're falling apart. Sometimes it means you're expanding.

The deeper movement remains **Experience → Feeling → Threshold → Release → Form**. Positive affirmations are **optional**: never require visitors to feel good, deny difficult feelings, or supply personal information. THE UNDERCURRENT remains separate, as do THE WATCHERS, THE NOISE, THE BLOOM and THE HEART.

## What is implemented versus what remains a future expansion

**Implemented now:** thirteen individually routed rooms in one connected immersive corridor; editable positive categories, quotes and affirmations; room-specific colors and CSS-built scene objects; click/tap interactions, keyboard focus, original abstract canvas transformation, affirmation constellation and carry-a-thought within the current page session; original artwork slots and clear placeholders; existing Bloom gallery preserved.

**Future richer environments:** fully free-roaming WebGL spaces, nested infinite-zoom worlds, recorded or spatial audio, and unique bespoke 3D architecture for every room. The first iteration uses interactive 2.5D/CSS installations and lightweight canvas effects, without promising capabilities it has not implemented.
