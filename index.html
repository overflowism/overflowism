✳ THE ARTIST / THE STORY

Some people keep diaries.
I built worlds.

When experience can no longer be contained, it becomes creation.

Overflowism began as a way to give feelings somewhere to live. Through original paintings and imagined spaces, the gallery explores the beautiful, difficult and contradictory parts of being human.

This museum will keep changing as new artwork arrives, new emotions emerge, and new rooms are discovered.
