# OVERFLOWISM — FOUNDATIONAL CREATIVE DIRECTION

**Original artistic manifesto — Marcela Thomas, 2026**

This document is the governing artistic foundation for the Overflowism movement and its website, `overflowism.lol`. It is not limited to the present emotional atlas or any single gallery, experience, medium, color palette, diagnosis, or aesthetic.

## Definition

Overflowism is an artistic movement born from the moment an internal or external experience becomes too intense to remain contained within the self.

## The foundational process

**Experience → Feeling → Threshold → Release → Form**

## Central statement

> "I create what I can't contain."

## Non-negotiable creative principles

- Overflowism is not limited to sadness, mental health, heartbreak, or emotional suffering.
- Joy, beauty, wonder, desire, spirituality, nature, music, travel, and love can overflow too.
- Intensity defines overflow, not the experience's source.
- The mind, body, and spirit are the first vessel; art becomes the second.
- The feeling chooses the form. There is no single required aesthetic or medium.
- Emotional truth takes priority over conventional beauty or perfection.
- Each gallery world should express different possibilities of overflow while remaining part of one artistic movement.

## Guidance for all future website work

These implementation notes explain how to preserve the manifesto without narrowing it:

1. **Lead with the artistic movement.** Present Overflowism first as an open-ended creative practice and movement. The emotional atlas is one expression within it, not its definition or its outer boundary.
2. **Honor the full range of intensity.** Do not frame overflow as inherently negative, clinical, traumatic, or tragic. Leave space for exuberance, sensuality, curiosity, celebrations, spirituality, nature, travel, music, connection, and feelings not yet named.
3. **Keep the process legible.** Where useful, content and interactions may explore Experience → Feeling → Threshold → Release → Form. This is a creative framework, not a mandatory visitor journey or rigid formula for making art.
4. **Make room for varied forms.** The platform should be able to host paintings alongside future poetry, written fragments, sound, photography, video, performance documentation, immersive environments, or other media chosen by the artist. No single style or technology defines Overflowism.
5. **Preserve existing worlds without making them exhaustive.** THE WATCHERS, THE NOISE, THE BLOOM, THE HEART and THE UNDERCURRENT are part of the expanding gallery. The platform must permit new worlds and chapters; no visitor should be required to identify with a diagnosis or a specific emotion to enter.
6. **Respect artistic truth and original work.** Do not rewrite the movement's definition, alter an artwork, claim a placeholder is original, attribute a feeling to a painting as fact without the artist's input, or trade emotional truth for conventional polish.
7. **Keep the invitation open.** Visitors can arrive through the mind-based entrance or choose to explore freely. Emotional tags are optional navigation, never clinical assessment.

## Creative review before changing the site

Before introducing a homepage rewrite, new world, room, visual treatment, audio experience, gallery navigation, or new content, ask:

- Does this honor intensity from **any** source, including joyful and beautiful ones?
- Does it preserve Experience → Feeling → Threshold → Release → Form without treating it as a clinical model?
- Does it leave the medium and aesthetic open to the artist?
- Does it avoid reducing Overflowism to mental health, suffering, or the current four worlds?
- Does it keep the artist's existing paintings and creative ownership intact?

If not, revise the proposal before merging.
