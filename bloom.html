HOW TO EXPLORE

Take your time.

Desktop: W / ↑ to walk forward, S / ↓ to step back, A/D or ←/→ to turn. Drag left or right on the scene to look around. E opens an artwork when nearby.

Phone: Use the on-screen arrows or drag the scene to look around. You can also click the artwork button when it appears.

This is an artist's imagined garden, not a virtual recreation of a real place. Motion can be paused at any time, and the artwork is also accessible through the gallery.
